How to read these results

  • Measurements are single-run microbenchmarks, not CI thresholds.
  • Comparisons are meaningful only for matching workloads and similar build profiles, hardware, and load.
  • Large movements without controlled repeat runs are signals for investigation, not confirmed regressions.
  • The full history and earlier baselines are maintained in the repository's research/BENCHMARK_BASELINE.md.
